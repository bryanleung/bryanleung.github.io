About

San Francisco Bay Area

I design products with complicated rules behind simple screens: course tools for instructors on Canvas LMS, and live games at Zynga and QuintAce. At Zynga I also led the design team on Harry Potter: Puzzles & Spells.

  • I work with product managers to understand the business requirements, and push for a better user experience within the time, resources and scope we have.
  • I understand the technical side, and work with engineers to find solutions that fit real engineering constraints.
  • Coherent products need clear alignment from everyone involved. I know how to get there, including when the people dynamics are difficult.

I work in Figma and Unity, and more recently with Claude to get to a testable prototype faster.
