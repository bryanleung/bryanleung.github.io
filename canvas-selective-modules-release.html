Selective Modules Release

Assigning 10 course materials to the right students went from 150 clicks to 6.

Product
Canvas LMS
Role
Senior Designer
Team
1 product manager, 6 engineers
Scope
Design, research, workshops
Year
2024

The problem

Instructors want to give different students different material: harder practice for an advanced student, or content hidden from a group it doesn't suit. Canvas allowed this one material at a time, and it was slow enough that instructors avoided it.

Assigning one material to one student took about 15 clicks across three pages. After saving, the instructor clicked Back twice and started again. Ten materials meant 150 clicks.

It would be great if I could give Modules unique gating rules specific to individual students or groups or sections within the course, similar to how I can create unique assignment due dates for different students and sections.

That left two questions to solve:

  • How can instructors assign materials in bulk, quickly?
  • How can moving from one material to the next take fewer steps?

Testing two designs

I designed two approaches and tested them as interactive prototypes in Maze, posted to the Canvas Community. Each participant was randomly given one prototype and the same task: assign an assignment to a student.

  • Permission modal. A new pattern modeled on sharing permissions, replacing the older UI.
  • Assign To tray. The existing component, extended to more places in Canvas. The safer option for both feasibility and familiarity.

The tray had the higher success rate and the shorter time, and engineers confirmed it would cost less to build. We went with the tray.

Assignable modules

Instructors already group materials into modules: assignments, quizzes, pages and media. We made the module itself assignable to students and sections, and everything inside it inherits the assignment.

An assignment has three parts: who it is for, when it is due, and when it is available. Due dates and availability stay on each material. Supporting them at module level would have been too complex, and instructors confirmed they didn't need it.

Quicker access

Assign To is now one menu option away on the Modules page and the Summary page.

Logic over interface

The interface changes are deliberately small so instructors have nothing new to learn. Most of the design work was in the rules: for example, whether assigning to a section overrides a setting already made for one student in it.

Results

Clicks to assign 10 materials
150 to 6
Reduction in effort
96%

I can't wait to see this feature enabled! I have been hoping for a feature like this since I started using Canvas back in 2020.

I feel that this will solve a lot of problems in cross listing some of our courses. Teachers will love being able to assign a different module to sections in their course.

Reflection

  • I designed a Modules page that shows who is assigned to what. It was out of scope for this release.
  • Parent and child assignments create many edge cases. We covered the ones we found, and I expect we missed a few.
